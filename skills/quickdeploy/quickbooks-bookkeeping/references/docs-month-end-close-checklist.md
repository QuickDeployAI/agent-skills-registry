## Close the books each month

1. Categorize or match every bank/credit-card transaction (For Review must be empty).
2. Reconcile each bank and credit-card account against its statement (**Settings → Reconcile**); resolve every discrepancy, never force-adjust.
3. Review A/R aging (invoices) and A/P aging (bills) for anything overdue or duplicated.
4. Verify undeposited funds is empty or explained.
5. Attach missing receipts to expenses over the business's receipt threshold.
6. Run P&L, Balance Sheet, and Statement of Cash Flows; compare to prior month.
7. Set the closing date (**Settings → Advanced → Close the books**) with a password so closed periods can't drift.
