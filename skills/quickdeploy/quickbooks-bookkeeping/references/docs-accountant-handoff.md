## Prepare the accountant packet

1. Confirm the close checklist above is complete for the period.
2. Export: P&L, Balance Sheet, Statement of Cash Flows, A/R aging, A/P aging, and the general ledger for the period (each report's Export button → CSV/Excel).
3. List open questions (uncategorized rows parked in *Ask My Accountant*, unusual swings, new accounts added).
4. Invite the accountant (**Settings → Manage users → Accounting firms**) instead of sharing credentials.
