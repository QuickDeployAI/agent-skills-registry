# Reminder templates

Drafts for each ladder stage. Fill placeholders; never invent amounts or
dates — pull them from the aging data. Every send is confirm-gated.

## Stage 1 — friendly nudge (1–15 days)

> Subject: Invoice {invoice_no} — friendly reminder
>
> Hi {first_name}, just a quick note that invoice {invoice_no} for
> {amount} was due on {due_date}. You can pay here: {payment_link}.
> If it's already on its way, please ignore this!

## Stage 2 — second notice (16–30 days)

> Subject: Invoice {invoice_no} is now {days_late} days past due
>
> Hi {first_name}, invoice {invoice_no} for {amount} (due {due_date})
> is still open. Pay here: {payment_link}. If cash timing is the issue,
> reply and we can set up a payment plan.

## Stage 3 — firm notice (31–60 days)

> Subject: Action needed: invoice {invoice_no} ({days_late} days past due)
>
> Hi {first_name}, despite earlier reminders, invoice {invoice_no} for
> {amount} remains unpaid since {due_date}. Please pay by {deadline} or
> reply today to arrange a call. {owner_name} is copied here.

## Stage 4 — owner escalation (60+ days)

Not a customer email. Produce an internal summary: full invoice list,
payment attempts and failure codes, contact history, customer's lifetime
value, and a recommended next step (final notice, payment plan, write-off,
or collections agency). The owner decides.
