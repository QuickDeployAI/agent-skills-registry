## Profit and Loss

1. Open **Reports → Profit and Loss**.
2. Set the period and the accounting method (cash vs accrual) the business actually uses — do not mix methods across periods.
3. Compare to the prior period and prior year; investigate any line that moved more than 20%.

## Statement of Cash Flows

1. Open **Reports → Statement of Cash Flows** for the same period.
2. Reconcile the ending cash with the bank balances before sharing.
3. Flag operating cash flow that is negative while P&L shows profit — usually receivables growing faster than collections.
